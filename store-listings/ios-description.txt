Check item values, compare trade offers and choose your next fishing rod with Fisch Values Calculator. This unofficial companion brings a trade checker, rod guide, fish sale calculator, event timers and codes into one place.

Look up an item before making an offer, compare two rods before an upgrade, or explore how weight and mutations change a catch's estimated sale value.

TRADE CALCULATOR AND WFL CHECKER
Add items to both sides of a proposed trade and compare their listed totals. Review the Win, Fair or Lose assessment, then adjust the offer to explore alternatives. Whether you are considering one item or a bundle, use the comparison to understand the difference between what you give and what you receive.

The WFL result is a reference based on available quotes. Consider item details and demand before deciding whether an offer works for you.

FISCH VALUE LIST
Search for an item by name or browse categories including fish, rod skins, boats, bobbers, gliders and lanterns. Check available trading values and see when a quote is missing. Look up an unfamiliar item before adding it to a proposed trade.

Use the catalogue as a companion while exploring your collection or planning your next exchange. Not every catalogue item is tradeable, and not every tradeable item has a published value.

ROD GUIDE AND SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARISON
Choosing a fishing rod involves more than one stat. Compare luck, lure speed, control, resilience, line distance and maximum weight. Sort the rod list by the attribute you want to explore and compare two rods side by side.

If you are looking for the best rod for your needs, use the comparison to weigh the differences yourself. A rod with greater luck may offer a different balance of control or resilience.

FISH VALUE AND MUTATION CALCULATOR
Estimate how much a catch could sell for to an NPC in C$. Select a fish, enter its weight and choose supported mutations to explore how those inputs affect the calculation. Compare different catch details without confusing a merchant sale estimate with a player trade quote.

Fish sale value and trading value answer different questions. The sale calculator estimates in-game cash; the trade calculator compares available item quotes.

ADMIN ABUSE AND BLACK MARKET TIMERS
View countdowns for Admin Abuse, the Black Market and weekly updates using the schedule information available in the app. Check the accompanying notes for timing uncertainty when planning your next session.

Schedules may be community-reported or incomplete, and the game developers can change event times. Treat the timers as planning references rather than guaranteed start times.

FISCH CODES
Browse the available code list and copy a code to try in the game. Codes may expire or have redemption requirements. Rewards and availability are controlled by the game developers, and the companion app does not redeem codes on your behalf.

PLAN YOUR NEXT SESSION
Start with the question you want to answer: what is this item worth, how does this rod compare, or what could this fish sell for? Open the relevant tool, review the available information and use it alongside your own experience in the game.

When quotes are missing, review the incomplete value result before trading. An unpriced item may still matter to players. Use the available figures as context when weighing up the whole trade offer.

ABOUT THIS COMPANION
Values are estimates, not guaranteed prices. Actual offers depend on item details, demand and the players involved. Missing quotes or game changes can affect results. A comparison does not guarantee profit, acceptance or a completed trade.
